# Content and confidentiality governance

Public repository means every branch, history, draft PR, artifact, fixture and asset is public. No private data or prohibited content may enter it. Private source review is external to the repo; do not copy raw records or private backups.

Class A: potentially public official titles, general responsibilities, public credentials, published research, granted publicly disclosed patents, authorized résumé-level accomplishments and general methodologies. Verify accuracy, attribution and publication approval.
Class B: professional case studies, technical decisions, project scope, engineering deliverables, metrics and outcomes. Require abstraction, verification and explicit item-level publication authorization; employer authorization when applicable.
Class C: confidential identifiers, products, equipment specs/layouts, proprietary drawings, internal photographs/documents, supplier details, production records/datasets, costs, undisclosed schedules, proprietary parameters and solutions, private records. Prohibited. Redrawing or anonymizing does not grant permission.

Workflow: Select → Abstract → Sanitize → Independently Reconstruct → Verify → Approve → Publish. Do not automatically synchronize private records. Explicit approval of content architecture is not approval of employer content.

Evidence types: professional experience (verified/authorized), published research/inventions (verified public references), independent demonstration (original work), illustrative/simulated results (hypothetical). Label simulations and assumptions at point of use. Never imply simulated output is an actual employment result. Use general principles, fictional scenarios, synthetic datasets and independent architectures. Check licensing and citations. Review personal contact and résumé details before release. Do not include private approval evidence in public metadata.

## Machine-enforced public record boundary
Only previously publication-authorized records may enter src/content. Strict schemas accept publicationStatus: approved exclusively; this field is a declaration after external review, not proof or a workflow override. Never commit drafts, private approval evidence, or prohibited materials. Evidence classifications separate public research, invention and credentials from authorized professional experience, independent demonstrations and illustrative simulations. References must be public HTTPS URLs; verify sources manually. Synthetic unit-test fixtures remain outside content collections. Build checks can identify structural problems, not employer authorization or confidentiality leaks in prose.

PP-005B credential metadata distinguishes owner-confirmed qualifications from public-reference verification. Only credential records marked owner-confirmed may omit a public proof URL; this status does not assert independent verification. Preserve approved year/month precision rather than fabricate day-level dates. Owner approval of general professional wording does not authorize confidential project claims or establish employer permission for material requiring it. Launch review remains mandatory.
## PP-008 approval loop
From PP-008 the owner reviews complete public wording in a private draft outside this repository and approves it once, as exact items, before any commit. Approved wording for the one-page résumé lives in src/lib/portfolio.ts and may change only with fresh owner approval. The page describes the owner's general way of working and names no customer, supplier, product, machine, project, figure or internal document; generic examples come from the owner's own role description. Class B and Class C rules above remain in force for any future addition.
